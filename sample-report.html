What your monthly report looks like

Short, in plain words, and it only ever contains things that actually happened. It arrives as a text or an email — whichever you prefer.

From Enoch · 1st of the month · to the owner

Morning — quick note on the website for Example Home Services.

11tapped your number
4sent an enquiry
137people looked

Eleven people tapped your phone number straight off the site, and four filled in the form. Those four went to your inbox as they came in, so you will have seen them already.

Busiest day was the 14th, which was a Saturday. Most people are finding you on their phone, not a computer — that is normal and the site is built for it.

What I changed this month: put your two new photos on, updated the hours for the holiday weekend, and fixed a line on the pricing section you asked about on the 9th. All of it the same day you asked.

Nothing needs doing from you. If anything changes — hours, prices, a new service, an offer you want running — just text me and it will be live that day.

Cheers,
Enoch

Why it is these three numbers

  • Tapped your number and sent an enquiry are people trying to reach you. That is the only thing that turns into work.
  • People looked is there for context, not for show. On its own it means very little.
  • You will not get bounce rates, session durations or anything else you did not ask for. If a number does not help you decide something, it does not belong in the note.

What it will never contain

  • A number I cannot back up.
  • Work I did not actually do. If nothing was asked for that month, the note says so.
  • Anything designed to make a quiet month look busy.
